Favorite Movies

Some movies that I enjoy are:

    • Tangled tells the story of Rapunzel, a young princess with magical long blonde hair who tries to leave her secret tower with the help of outlaw Flynn Rider.

  • Hidden Figures

    • Hidden Figures is the story of a team of talented African American women mathematicians who worked as human "computers" at NASA during the 1950s and 1960s. (click the image below to learn more)

  • Remarkably Bright Creatures

    • Remarkably Bright Creatures is a heartwarming story about a lonely 70-year-old widow who forms an unlikely friendship with a giant Pacific octopus at a local aquarium.
